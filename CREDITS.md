# Credits

Monrovia Life: Streets, a LibGamer community game.

## Third-party assets
- **Ultimate Animated Character Pack** by [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatedanimatedcharacter.html)
  (CC0 1.0). The 3D people and their animations (idle, walk, run, jump, sit down,
  fall). Recoloured in game. Licence and details: `third_party/quaternius-ultimate-animated-character-pack/`.

## Original art
Everything else is original and generated by the project's own scripts.

- 3D city, district and vehicles: `tools/build_city.py`, `tools/export_district.py`,
  `tools/make_sprites.py` (Blender).
- Lappa and head-wrap attachments for the people: `proto3d/character.gd`.
- Earlier home-made rigged person (no longer used in game): `tools/make_characters.py`.
- Street dressing (sidewalks, markings, lights, poles, signs, props, palms, flags):
  generated in `proto3d/street_dresser.gd`.
- Materials: shaders in `proto3d/*.gdshader`.
- 2D sprites: rendered from the Blender models by `tools/make_sprites.py`.
