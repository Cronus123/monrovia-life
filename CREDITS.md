# Credits

Monrovia Life: Streets, a LibGamer community game.

## Third-party assets
- **Ultimate Animated Character Pack** by [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatedanimatedcharacter.html)
  (CC0 1.0). The 3D people and their animations (idle, walk, run, jump, sit down,
  fall). Recoloured in game. Licence and details: `third_party/quaternius-ultimate-animated-character-pack/`.

- **Car Kit 3.1** by [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit) (CC0 1.0). The cars, taxi,
  police car, SUV, sports car, pickup, minibus, box truck and armoured van, plus the crash
  debris (bumpers, doors, tyres). Re-worked by `tools/make_vehicles.py` (real size, see-through
  glass, interiors, lights, paint, TAXI / POLICE / SECURE CASH liveries - no real brands).
  Licence and source: `third_party/kenney-car-kit/`. The keke is our own model, built by the same script.

## Map data (1:1 Monrovia city, `proto3d/main_city.tscn`)
- **OpenStreetMap**: roads, buildings, coastline, water, land use and place names are
  (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL 1.0),
  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright. The generated city tiles (`proto3d/city/`) are a
  derived database: if you share them, share them under the ODbL too and keep this attribution.
  The game shows "Map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL" on screen.
- **Elevation**: Terrain Tiles on AWS Open Data (Terrarium encoding), derived here from SRTM
  (NASA, public domain). https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/
- Downloaded by `tools/fetch_map_data.py`; the city is built by `tools/build_osm_city.py`.

## Original art
Everything else is original and generated by the project's own scripts.

- 3D city and district: `tools/build_city.py`, `tools/export_district.py`,
  `tools/make_sprites.py` (Blender). The keke: `tools/make_vehicles.py`.
- Lappa and head-wrap attachments for the people: `proto3d/character.gd`.
- Earlier home-made rigged person (no longer used in game): `tools/make_characters.py`.
- Street dressing (sidewalks, markings, lights, poles, signs, props, palms, flags):
  generated in `proto3d/street_dresser.gd`.
- Materials: shaders in `proto3d/*.gdshader`.
- Sound effects, ambience and music (menu theme, three radio stations): synthesised from scratch
  by `tools/make_audio.py` (no samples or third-party audio).
- Shop and restaurant names are made up; any resemblance to real businesses is coincidental.
- 2D sprites: rendered from the Blender models by `tools/make_sprites.py`.
